How it works

✈️ Telegram

We check the public t.me page, then fragment.com for names sold or on auction. Telegram needs 5+ characters; shorter names are only sold on Fragment.

📸 Instagram

We query Instagram's public profile lookup. "Not found" usually means free, but names can be reserved or belong to banned accounts.

⚖️ Fair use

Requests are throttled and cached server-side to stay polite to both platforms. Always confirm by claiming the name in the app.
